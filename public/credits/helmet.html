MartisDux artwork credits

The loading screen uses Astronaut Helmet by Medhatelo under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0. The MartisDux website team converted, resized, and compressed the model and textures for the web. The desktop app uses that same converted file and adds new motion to the loader.

The Home astronaut is a generated illustration based on a render of that helmet. The Mars globe uses the app’s NASA, JPL, and USGS imagery, credited in Data & sources.
