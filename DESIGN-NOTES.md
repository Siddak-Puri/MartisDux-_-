# Main explorer design notes — 9 November 2026

This pass reviewed NASA's official 2024 and 2025 Space Apps winner summaries.
The following are design lessons inferred for MartisDux, not claims that its UI
copies a winner or that NASA has endorsed or judged this application.

- **Landsat Connect, AsturExplorers (2024, Best Mission Concept):** NASA describes
  intuitive satellite tracking and access to reflectance data. Applied here as
  direct map actions and quick access to scientific sources.
- **G.R.O.W., Team I.O. (2024, Local Impact):** NASA highlights accessible GIS
  communication. Applied here as short control labels, visible layer switches,
  coordinate input, and explanations kept in Data/Help.
- **SpaceGenes+ (2025, Best Use of Science):** NASA describes an interactive
  research dashboard. Applied here as a focused working surface with persistent
  routes and source-aware export, rather than a promotional landing page.

Sources:
- https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-international-space-apps-challenge-announces-2024-global-winners/
- https://www.nasa.gov/learning-resources/stem-engagement-at-nasa/nasa-announces-2025-international-space-apps-challenge-global-winners/

The MartisDux text wordmark, orange/cream palette and locally bundled typography
are retained. The default workflow is map → pin → destination → route → save/export.
Terrain and rover photos have direct buttons. The HUD simulator is deferred.
