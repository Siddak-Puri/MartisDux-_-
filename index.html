                HOW IT WORKS

                Quick guide

                Move around
                Drag to spin Mars. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
                Find a place
                Type in the search bar, or press /.
                Plan a walk
                Open Walk. Pick a start, then a destination. You get a GO or NO-GO.
                Change a route
                Drag pin A or B on the map.
                Run it live
                Press Start the walk, then throw a problem at it: solar storm, suit leak, injury. The map makes the call and shows the way home.
                Storm shelter
                Shows where the ground hides part of the sky and blocks radiation.
                Walk it in 3D
                Use W A S D to move and drag to look. Esc goes back.
                Rover photos
                Open Photos to look around where Perseverance and Curiosity stood.
                Start again
                Use the globe control to reset the map, or choose Home in the sidebar.

                All maps and photos are real NASA mission data.
